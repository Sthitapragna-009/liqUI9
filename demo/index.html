Liquid Glass and Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, for designers building on the web, in Figma and in SwiftUI. Drag the lens across the title.

The material

Glass bends what's behind it, catches light on its rim and adapts to the content. Regular is the default; Clear sits over rich media with a dimming layer; Tinted is reserved for the one primary action.

Regularbars, buttons, menus

Frosted and refracted; labels stay monochrome.

Clearover photos and video

Dim behind clear glass: 35%

Tintedthe primary action only

Colour the glass body, not the label.

Buttons

Four styles signal hierarchy; sizes stay consistent within a set; roles change colour, never size. Press and hold to feel the squish.

Controls

Content-layer controls stay solid at rest. The moving part becomes glass while you interact: the segmented control's thumb glides and stretches, the slider thumb turns to glass while dragged.

Data under glass

The chart lives in the content layer; a clear lens follows your pointer and magnifies the line, with the value on a glass label. The card itself stays solid.

Screens

Every interaction from the motion piece Designing UI components with Liquid Glass, live: seven screens of content-layer cards with glass only where it belongs. Click, drag, scrub and hold, or press Watch to play the screen through.

Bars, and where glass doesn't go

Scroll the phones. A floating glass tab bar minimises as you scroll, and a scroll edge effect separates the top controls from content. Glass stays on the functional layer; cards are content.
